FeatureGen / architecture guide

Ingestion flow, stage by stage

How catalog metadata becomes governed, searchable context for safe feature discovery. Select a stage to inspect its purpose, controls, and banking example.

Boundary: ingestion processes metadata—schemas, glossaries, table and column facts, and relationship claims. Customer transaction rows remain in the bank-controlled data platform.

Primary ingestion path

Invalid row → quarantine

Malformed identities, duplicate records, conflicts, or structural failures are retained with a reason but do not enter the graph or authority layer.

Large change → hold for review

A material source reduction or unexpected replacement is held before it can remove or alter trusted catalog context.
